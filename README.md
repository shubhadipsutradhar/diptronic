# ⚡ DipTronic : Unified Tech Exchange & Storefront

> A modern, multi-role e-commerce platform and trade-in exchange for electrical and technical appliances.

Diptronic is a feature-rich web platform designed to provide a seamless online shopping experience alongside an innovative device trade-in and exchange workflow. Featuring an intuitive interface, role-based access portals, a dynamic valuation engine, and secure checkout options, Diptronic makes buying, selling, and trading technical appliances simple, efficient, and secure.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🛒 **Comprehensive Product Catalog & Condition Tiers**
  - Browse structured categories including Phones, Laptops, Cameras, Audio, Wearables, Tablets, Gaming Consoles, and Smart Home.
  - Filter inventory instantly by condition tiers: *Brand New*, *Refurbished*, and *Direct Sale*.

- 🔄 **Instant Price Calculator & Trade-In Engine**
  - Calculate instant cash offers based on cosmetic quality grade and battery health percentage.
  - Track device submissions through a transparent 4-stage lifecycle workflow from intake to live store publication.

- 🔒 **Multi-Role Portals (RBAC)**
  - **Market & Trade-In:** Public storefront for shopping and submitting devices.
  - **Partner Hub:** Secure workspace for partner stores to review submissions and place competitive cash offers.
  - **Admin Review Desk:** Auditing console for final quality verification and publishing approved inventory.

- 💳 **Secure Payment Options**
  - Integrated checkout supporting UPI QR code scanning, Credit/Debit card inputs, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

- 🔍 **Smart Product Search & Filters**
  - Instantly search device titles and technical specifications in real-time.

- 📱 **Fully Responsive Design**
  - Optimized for smooth navigation across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile devices.

- ⚡ **Modern UI/UX & Dark Mode**
  - Clean interface combining Space Grotesk and Syne fonts with a built-in light/dark theme toggle.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Deliver a dual-purpose platform combining traditional e-commerce with a reverse-logistics device trade-in market.
- Provide automated instant device valuations backed by partner store bidding.
- Maintain a fast, highly responsive, and accessible user interface.
- Implement robust role-based access control for users, partners, and administrators.

---

## 💡 Why Diptronic?

Diptronic bridges the gap between retail electronics and device recycling. By uniting a clean storefront, instant trade-in calculators, multi-role partner workflows, and flexible payment methods into a single ecosystem, users enjoy a fast, reliable, and engaging shopping experience across all devices.
